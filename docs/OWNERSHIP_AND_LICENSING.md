# Ownership and dependency review

The user's instruction authorises adaptation of their existing repository; it is not independent evidence of every contributor's IP assignment. Verify the ownership chain, contractors' terms, included artwork, report wording and redistribution rights before commercial licensing.

The original report engine is pinned with provenance. Existing third-party notices and the LGPL licence text for `heic-to` remain distributed under `public/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt` and `public/licenses/`. The HEIC module is lazy-loaded separately. LGPL and codec dependencies may require source/relinking information and notices; a proprietary top-level notice does not override those obligations.

`npm run licenses` creates a dependency inventory from the exact lock file with names, versions, licence identifiers and integrity hashes. Review exceptions and actual licence terms; neither an inventory nor a zero-vulnerability audit constitutes legal clearance.

Before sale, approve subscription terms, customer data ownership/export/termination clauses, support limits, liability, subcontractor/privacy terms and the rights to use customer logos. No executed customer agreement or independent legal approval is created by this repository.
